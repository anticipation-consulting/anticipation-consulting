Data Science & Modeling

The hands-on work itself. We build, validate, and interpret the models that answer your hardest empirical questions — and we document them so the answer holds up long after we've gone.

Discuss a project → All services
The difference

Whoever frames the problem also does the work.

Most consultancies scope an analysis and subcontract the doing — the senior name on the proposal is rarely the person who touches the data. Here, they are the same person. The scientist who frames your question writes the code, interrogates the assumptions, and signs the conclusion.

That continuity is what makes results trustworthy. Nothing is lost in handoff, no assumption goes unexamined, and there is one person who can explain every decision in the pipeline — to your board, your regulator, or an opposing expert.

Methods

The right tool for the question — not the fashionable one.

01 Predictive modeling

Forecasting and classification with an honest accounting of uncertainty, error, and the cost of being wrong. Calibrated, not just accurate.

02 Causal inference

Quasi-experiments, difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, and matched designs that separate genuine effects from coincidence.

03 Network analysis

Structure, influence, community, and flow in social, market, and infrastructure networks — including backbone extraction and significance.

04 Text & language models

NLP and LLM-based measurement with rigorous evaluation: validity, reliability, and error analysis — not a benchmark screenshot.

05 Statistical analysis

Experimental design, hypothesis testing, and regression done with the assumptions stated and checked, the way a reviewer would demand.

06 Reproducible engineering

Versioned code, documented data lineage, and pipelines that a third party can re-run and arrive at the same number.

How an engagement runs

From question to defensible answer.

01 — Frame

Define & scope

We pin down the decision the analysis must serve, the data available, and the ways an answer could mislead. You get a written scope before any commitment.

02 — Build

Model & validate

We construct, stress-test, and cross-check the model — documenting assumptions and running the analyses that could prove it wrong.

03 — Translate

Interpret & deliver

Results become a clear narrative with decision-grade figures, plus the technical appendix for anyone who wants to check the work.

04 — Transfer

Hand off & support

Code, documentation, and a walkthrough so your team can maintain, extend, and defend the work after the engagement ends.

Every number we deliver comes with the question “how could this be wrong?” already asked — and answered.
