Privacy & security.

We hold client work to a high standard of confidentiality, so the site holds itself to the same one: it collects nothing, tracks nothing, and calls no third party.

Privacy

What this site collects: essentially nothing.

This is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers, pixels, or ad networks, and loads no third-party resources — fonts and every other asset are served from this domain, so your browser never contacts another company to render a page. There is nothing here to consent to, which is why you saw no cookie banner.

Server logs

Our host (Netlify) processes each request to deliver the page and may keep standard, short-lived access logs — typically IP address, timestamp, and user-agent — for security and operations. We do not use those logs to profile or track visitors, and we do not combine them with anything else.

When you contact us

If you email or message us, we use what you send only to respond. We won't add you to a mailing list and we won't share it. Email is hosted on Proton Mail; for sensitive or privileged matters you can encrypt to our PGP key or reach us over Signal. Links to outside services (LinkedIn, GitHub, Bluesky, Signal) contact those services only if you choose to click them.

No third parties

The site makes no calls to content-delivery networks, font services, comment systems, or form handlers. A privacy check runs on every build, and fails it if any third-party resource is ever introduced — so it stays this way.

Security & responsible disclosure

We welcome good-faith reports of security or privacy issues. Email contact@anticipationconsulting.com (encrypt to our PGP key for anything sensitive), or use the machine-readable /.well-known/security.txt. Please give us a reasonable chance to remediate before public disclosure. We won't pursue researchers who act in good faith and avoid privacy violations, data destruction, or service disruption.

How the site is built

The site is open source, and its security posture is visible in our repository: a strict Content-Security-Policy, HSTS, self-hosted fonts, and an automated quarterly review of dependencies. We try to practice what we advise.

Questions or changes

Questions about privacy? Get in touch. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here.
