What we do

Evidence designed for scrutiny — at every step.

From the data we start with to the conclusion you act on, every stage is built to hold up — in the boardroom, in the courtroom, and on the record.

01 Audited data

We clean, validate, and stress-test the data first — surfacing the gaps, bias, and leakage that quietly break an analysis.

02 Reproducible methods

Versioned code and documented data lineage ship with every result, so you (or a third party) can re-run it and reach the same answer.

03 Interpretable results

No black boxes. We show what drives each finding and how far to trust it — so it survives a second look.

04 Accessible conclusions

Findings turned into narrative and visuals a decision-maker can act on — a board, a regulator, or a jury.

How we work

Find the question. Build the evidence. Make it clear. Hand it over.

01 — Discover

Find the real question

The first conversation is free. Before any code, we find what you didn't know to look for. Discovery produces a scoping memo that pins down the question, defines what a useful result looks like, and flags feasibility concerns before money is committed to execution.

02 — Build

Build the evidence

A written plan of work before anything starts. Predictive, causal, network, and language models — constructed, validated, and iterated with you in the loop. No black-box delivery.

03 — Translate

Make it clear

Findings tied to specific recommended actions and rendered into narrative and visuals the decision-maker can follow — a board, a regulator, or a jury.

04 — Deliver

Hand it over

Every deliverable ships with documented code, data lineage, and a handoff briefing. A named owner on your side takes custody. The analysis is yours to re-run, update, and extend.
